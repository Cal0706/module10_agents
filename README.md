Mini-Project Tax Office Appointment Helper

For my mini-project, I created a tax office appointment assistant that helps customers check available appointment times on different days. The assistant uses a tool to look up appointment availability and keeps a history of the conversation. The appointment schedule and responses are mock data, meaning they are examples rather than actual customer information or real appointments. Requests to book, cancel, or reschedule an appointment are passed to a human employee because the assistant cannot make changes to the schedule. If the assistant was using real customer data, there could be privacy risks, incorrect appointment information or unauthorized access to sensitive information.


For my Week 12 mini-project, I upgraded my appointment assistant to use AI to decide when exactly to call tools. The assistant successfully passed appointment cancellation requests to the front desk instead of handling them automatically. The toolbox also checks that customers provide the necessary information before submitting booking requests. I added instructions to help the assistant understand when to ask for missing information rather than making assumptions. Appointment requests now wait for staff approval before they are finalized.
